# Changelog

## [1.0.1] - 2026-09-28

- First release in the Store: the temperature and its own mark on the wings when nothing else wants the notch, the weather on the shelf with today's high and low and the next five hours, and a row in the lock screen's status widgets.
- Its settings are Droppy's own grouped page, with the system's pop-up menus for the unit and the interval, and a Choose button beside each city the search finds.
- It asks for no location permission. The city comes from your internet address, checked against your time zone, or from the one you name in its settings.
